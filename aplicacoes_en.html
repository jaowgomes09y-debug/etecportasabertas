Digital Content Manager

What they do: plan, organize, publish, and track content on digital channels.

Education: can involve Marketing, Communication, IT, Content Production, or related courses.

Where it's applied: in digital marketing agencies, news portals, tech companies, and educational institutions to manage websites, corporate blogs, and social media engagement strategies.

Reference salary: social media analyst: average of R$ 2,872.71/month, with a minimum of R$ 2,151.19 and a maximum of R$ 5,078.78 (Salary Portal/CAGED, 2026).

How do you choose a career?

Observe which activities you enjoy: programming, analyzing data, creating interfaces, explaining content, managing networks or protecting information. Then research education paths, build projects and learn the real requirements of job openings.
