IT Professionals

Technology depends on people with different knowledge, roles and responsibilities.

What is Information Technology?

Information Technology (IT) involves using computers, systems, networks, data and software to store, process, protect and transmit information. It is a broad field made up of professionals with different specialties.

Why are there so many careers?

A digital product goes through several stages: planning, development, design, testing, deployment, security, data analysis, support and communication. Each stage may involve specialized professionals.

Content Manager/Creator

In IT, a content manager or creator works on producing and organizing digital materials, turning technical subjects into texts, videos, posts, tutorials, courses and other formats. They can also analyze audiences, define goals, organize editorial calendars and monitor results.

Education and skills

There is no single mandatory education path for every IT career. Depending on the position, technical courses and degrees such as Computer Science, Information Systems, Systems Analysis and Development, Networks or related fields may be useful. A portfolio, projects, additional courses and practice can also make a difference.

Recommended schools and institutions

For those looking to qualify in the region and the state, renowned public and technical institutions stand out for IT education:

  • ETEC of Hortolândia: Free technical courses of excellence in the technology field.
  • FATEC (College of Technology): Public technology colleges in the state of São Paulo with free higher education courses (like ADS and IT Management).
  • SENAI and SENAC: Traditional institutions with a strong connection to the job market, offering technical and professional qualification courses.
