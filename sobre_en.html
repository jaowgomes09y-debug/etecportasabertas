The people behind technology

Technology comes to life through curious, creative people who are ready to collaborate.

What is Information Technology?

Information Technology (IT) brings together computers, systems, networks, data and software to store, process, protect and share information. It is a broad field with room for many different talents.

Why are there so many paths?

A digital product moves through planning, development, design, testing, deployment, security, data analysis, support and communication. Each stage makes room for people with different skills.

Content Manager/Creator

In IT, a content manager or creator works on producing and organizing digital materials, turning technical subjects into texts, videos, posts, tutorials, courses and other formats. They can also analyze audiences, define goals, organize editorial calendars and monitor results.

Education and skills

There is no single mandatory education path for every IT career. Depending on the position, technical courses and degrees such as Computer Science, Information Systems, Systems Analysis and Development, Networks or related fields may be useful. A portfolio, projects, additional courses and practice can also make a difference.

Where can you study for this career?

There is no single course called “Content Manager/Creator”. The career combines communication, marketing, technology and hands-on digital practice. These are useful starting points:

  • ETEC of Hortolândia — Systems Development: a free public technical course, generally lasting about 3 semesters and approximately 1,200 hours. It covers programming, web, databases and projects, helping students create technical content and digital products.
  • FATEC — Information Technology Management: a free public degree, generally lasting 6 semesters and around 2,400 hours. It covers management, projects, systems, data and processes, all useful for organizing digital content strategies and teams.
  • SENAC: short and technical courses in digital marketing, content production, communication and social media. Duration and workload vary by class, usually from a few weeks to a few months, with practical activities and portfolio work.
  • SENAI: professional courses in communication, digital tools, design and technology. Workload varies by course and commonly includes practical exercises focused on the job market.

A good combination: complete a technical course or degree, add content-focused courses and build a portfolio with posts, videos, scripts, texts or campaigns. Check each institution’s website to confirm applications, schedules, format and workload for the available class.

About salaries: values vary significantly according to position, experience, region, company, employment model and specialization. Therefore, salaries shown on the applications page are approximate references, not guarantees.
